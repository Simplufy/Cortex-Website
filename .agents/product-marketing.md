# Product Marketing Context

**Document version:** v2
**Last updated:** 2026-08-26

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Cortex installs AI agents inside the software service businesses already use.
**What it does:** We audit how the company operates, connect specialized agents to CRM, shop/field software, ads, inbox, and phone, then manage the workforce. Existing software stays the system of record.
**Product category:** Custom AI workforce / AI operations infrastructure
**Product type:** Implementation + managed operations
**Business model:** One-time implementation, then monthly managed AI operations. Free AI Operations Audit first.

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Established service businesses, typically well above early-stage. Auto, HVAC, pest, roofing, plumbing, electrical, windows, smart home, contracting, home services.
**Decision-makers:** Owners, CEOs, COOs, GMs, ops/marketing leads
**Primary use case:** People still copy, check, follow up, and report by hand across software they already pay for.

## Differentiation
Map the whole operation. Agents connected to existing tools. Not chatbots, not Zapier-plus-ChatGPT, not a rip-and-replace. Cloud, private, or hybrid. Managed after go-live.

## Brand Voice
Direct, practical, premium, operator-to-operator. No hype. No fake metrics. No em dashes.

## Goals
**Conversion action:** Get a Free AI Operations Audit
